# Stage Break

This file defines what is meant by “the stage breaks.”

The stage is the assumed background that lets ordinary physics describe events.

In ordinary conditions, the stage appears stable enough to use:

space  
time  
distance  
duration  
locality  
sequence  
curvature  
measurement  
objects  
events  

But at the fracture zone, the stage itself becomes part of the problem.

## The ordinary stage

Most physical descriptions assume some way to say:

where something is  
when something happens  
how far apart things are  
how long something lasts  
what counts as nearby  
what counts as before or after  
what counts as an object  
what counts as a frame  

This is the stage.

It does not have to mean empty space.

It means the structure that allows events to be placed, compared, measured, and described.

## Why the stage matters

If the stage is stable, the question can be:

What happens inside space and time?

But if the stage is unstable, the question changes.

The question becomes:

What allows space and time to exist as a usable frame at all?

## Where the stage breaks

The stage breaks at singularity-boundaries.

These include:

black hole interiors  
Big Bang boundary  
possible Big Crunch boundary  
extreme density  
extreme curvature  
Planck-scale regimes  

At these boundaries, the ordinary assumptions stop being safe.

Space may not remain smooth.

Time may not remain clock-like.

Locality may not remain clear.

Distance may not remain measurable in the ordinary way.

The frame may no longer separate cleanly from what happens inside it.

## What breaks

The stage break pressures several assumptions at once:

space as container  
time as external clock  
distance as already measurable  
locality as already defined  
sequence as already ordered  
curvature as already smooth  
objects as already separable  
events as already placeable  

The break is not only physical.

It is structural.

The model cannot simply ask what happens inside the stage if the stage is what fails.

## The hidden problem

A theory can accidentally smuggle in the stage.

It can use words like:

before  
after  
inside  
outside  
near  
far  
point  
distance  
change  
collapse  
expansion  
location  

before explaining what makes those words valid.

This repo treats that as a warning sign.

If the question is how spacetime emerges, then spacetime cannot be used as the unexplained starting point.

## Cleaner formulation

The stage break means:

the ordinary frame of space, time, locality, and measure cannot be assumed as already available.

So the deeper question is not:

What happened first in space?

The deeper question is:

What allows there to be a first, a where, and a space?

## Current position

The fracture zone shows where the stage fails.

The stage break names what fails.

The next problem is to ask what can remain before the stage returns.

## Bridge to the next file

If the beginning cannot be nothing, vacuum, empty space, or a point inside space, then the starting phrase must be cleaner.

The current candidate is:

**undifferentiated symmetry**

Not nothing.

Not empty space.

Not chaos.

Maximal sameness before difference.

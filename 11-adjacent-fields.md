## 1. Quantum gravity

Quantum gravity is the broad attempt to reconcile general relativity and quantum theory.

General relativity treats gravity as dynamical spacetime geometry.

Quantum theory treats physical systems through quantum states, probabilities, fields, measurements, and interactions.

The fracture appears where both must matter at once:

black hole interiors  
Big Bang boundary  
possible Big Crunch boundary  
extreme density  
extreme curvature  
Planck-scale regimes  

This repo is adjacent to quantum gravity because it asks where the overlap fails.

But this repo does not claim to solve quantum gravity.

## 2. Background independence

Many physical theories use a background structure.

Dark matter research is adjacent to the inversion-sediment hypothesis because dark matter is inferred through gravitational effects without corresponding visible matter.

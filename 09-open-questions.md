# Open Questions

This file holds the unanswered questions.

The purpose is to keep the repo honest.

We are not claiming completion.

We are identifying the fracture, preserving the knowns, and naming the live edges that still need work.

## Central open question

The central open question is:

**When the stage breaks, what remains stable enough to generate a stage again?**

Everything else branches from that.

## 1. What is the weave made of?

The current model points toward a possible weave beneath spacetime.

But the weave has not been defined yet.

Possible candidates include:

relation  
distinction  
recursive boundary  
causal order  
information structure  
probability structure  
constraint  
pattern  
something not yet named  

The repo has not yet answered which one is primary.

Current open question:

**What remains when space, time, locality, and metric cannot be assumed?**

## 2. Where does the ruler come from?

The model may have a candidate for order.

It may have a candidate for sequence.

It may have a candidate for a pre-time arrow.

But it does not yet have measure.

Open questions:

What gives amount?

What makes duration countable?

What makes distance measurable?

What makes curvature measurable?

What makes one interval different from another?

What allows ordered depth to become metric structure?

Current edge sentence:

**The arrow may be earned. The amount is still open.**

## 3. How does sequence become duration?

Sequence means one structure comes before another in an order.

Duration means measurable interval.

Those are not the same.

Open questions:

What turns ordered relation into elapsed time?

What makes a before / after structure measurable?

What makes a fold count as longer, shorter, earlier, or later?

What stabilizes recurrence enough to compare it against itself?

## 4. How does locality return?

Open questions:

What separates inside from outside?

What makes near and far meaningful?

What lets events become placeable?

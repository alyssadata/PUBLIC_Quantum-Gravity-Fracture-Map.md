# Fracture Zone

This file identifies the first break.

We are not starting with a proposed solution.

We are starting with the place where the existing frameworks stop fitting cleanly together.

## The known overlap problem

General relativity works.

Quantum theory works.

The problem appears where both are required at once.

That is the fracture zone.

## Where the fracture appears

The fracture appears under extreme conditions:

black hole interiors  
Big Bang boundary  
possible Big Crunch boundary  
extreme density  
extreme curvature  
Planck-scale regimes  

These are not ordinary cases inside an ordinary stage.

They are boundary conditions where the stage itself becomes unstable.

## The first fracture claim

The overlap does not fail only because gravity is strong.

The cleaner claim is:

**The overlap fails because the stage stops being safely separable.**

At the fracture zone, the ordinary separations begin to break:

space / time  
gravity / geometry  
location / relation  
sequence / duration  
inside / outside  
object / frame  
event / background  

## Why this matters

If spacetime is stable, physics can describe what happens inside it.

But if spacetime itself is unstable, then the question changes.

The question is no longer only:

What happens in space?

The earlier question is:

What remains when space is no longer a stable container?

The question is no longer only:

What happens over time?

The earlier question is:

What remains when time is no longer a stable clock?

## Singularity-boundaries

The first named fracture shape is:

**singularity-boundary**

A singularity-boundary is a condition where the ordinary stage fails or becomes undefined.

It is not just a place.

It is a limit where the usual frame can no longer safely hold:

distance  
duration  
locality  
smooth curvature  
ordinary sequence  
ordinary measurement  

## Current claim

The overlap between general relativity and quantum theory fails at singularity-boundaries because the stage itself stops being safely separable.

This does not mean the answer is known.

It means the first fracture has been located.

## What the fracture asks

If the stage breaks, what remains?

If time cannot be assumed, what gives sequence?

If sequence appears, what gives duration?

If locality fails, what makes locality return?

If spacetime is not fundamental, what generates spacetime?

If gravity is geometry, what is gravity before geometry is stable?

## The central question

**When the stage breaks, what remains stable enough to generate a stage again?**

That is the fracture zone.

# Unanswered Questions

When the stage breaks, what remains stable enough to generate a stage again?

Branches:

What is the weave made of?
Is it relation, information, distinction, causal order, recursive boundary, probability structure, or something else?

Where does the ruler come from?
You may have sequence, but you do not yet have measure. You may have before/after shape, but not interval, duration, metric, distance, or amount.

How does locality return?
If the singularity-boundary is all-at-once or nonlocal, what mechanism produces “here,” “there,” “before,” “after,” and “near”?

What does inversion conserve?
If sediment carries across cycles, what crosses the boundary? Amount? structure? information? gravitational effect? relational imprint?

What makes inversion happen?
“Existence/non-existence cannot share the center” is philosophically strong, but it is not yet a physical mechanism. It needs to be labeled either metaphysical engine, formal constraint, or physical hypothesis.

What would be measured differently?
The dark-matter bridge is promising as a place to look, but evidence like the Bullet Cluster supports separation between ordinary matter and dark matter through gravitational lensing; it does not by itself prove prior-cycle sediment. NASA’s Webb update says the Bullet Cluster’s gas and dark matter are separated, offering evidence for dark matter; ESA similarly describes gravitational lensing as revealing mass dominated by dark matter.

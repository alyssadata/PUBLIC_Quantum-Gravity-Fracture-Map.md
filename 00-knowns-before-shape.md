# Knowns Before Shape

This file holds the immobile base.

Before proposing any shape, model, mechanism, weave, inversion, sediment, or emergence chain, the knowns must be preserved.

The question is not:

What beautiful idea explains everything?

The question is:

What deeper structure could preserve what already works, explain where the overlap breaks, and avoid smuggling in spacetime before spacetime has been earned?

## Known 1: General relativity works where it works

General relativity successfully describes gravity as dynamical spacetime geometry.

Any deeper model must recover the tested successes of general relativity, including:

planetary motion  
gravitational lensing  
black holes  
gravitational waves  
time dilation  
large-scale cosmic structure  

A proposed solution fails if it destroys ordinary gravity where ordinary gravity is already measured.

## Known 2: Quantum theory works where it works

Quantum theory successfully describes small-scale physical behavior through quantum states, probabilities, fields, measurement structures, and interactions.

Any deeper model must preserve quantum behavior where it is already verified, including:

superposition  
uncertainty  
probability  
measurement effects  
field / particle behavior  
electromagnetic, weak, and strong interactions  

A proposed solution fails if it erases quantum behavior instead of explaining it.

## Known 3: Gravity is not optional

Gravity cannot be treated as an afterthought.

A valid deeper model must explain why gravity appears as spacetime curvature in the domains where general relativity works.

It must also explain what gravity is, or becomes, when spacetime is no longer safe to assume as a stable background.

## Known 4: Quantum behavior is not optional

Quantum behavior cannot be erased to make the model easier.

A valid deeper model must preserve the reality of quantum behavior while explaining how that behavior relates to spacetime, locality, time, and gravity.

## Known 5: The fracture appears where both sides must matter at once

The deepest pressure appears where general relativity and quantum theory are both required:

black hole interiors  
Big Bang boundary  
possible Big Crunch boundary  
extreme density  
extreme curvature  
Planck-scale regimes  

These are not ordinary locations inside a stable stage.

They are boundary conditions where the stage itself becomes unstable.

## Known 6: The stage cannot be assumed too early

Space, time, distance, locality, sequence, metric, and smooth curvature cannot be used as unexplained starting assumptions if the question is how spacetime itself emerges or breaks.

If the model begins by assuming a stage, it has not explained the stage.

So the early question is not:

What happens inside space?

The earlier question is:

What remains before space can hold an inside?

## Known 7: Time cannot be treated as already solved

Standard quantum theory requires a stable background time, causal frame, or evolution parameter.

General relativity makes spacetime geometry dynamic.

At the fracture, time cannot simply be assumed as an external clock.

A valid deeper model must explain how clock-time is recovered, or how time emerges from something more basic.

## Known 8: Locality must be recovered

The ordinary world is local.

Things appear here rather than there.

Events appear ordered.

Objects occupy frames.

Signals have structure.

Any deeper model must explain how locality returns if locality is not fundamental at the boundary.

## Known 9: Measure must be earned

Sequence is not enough.

Order is not yet duration.

Before / after shape is not yet interval.

A valid model must explain the ruler:

amount  
metric  
duration  
distance  
measurable interval  

The current edge is:

The arrow may be earned.

The amount is still open.

## Known 10: No complete quantum gravity theory is experimentally confirmed

There are serious existing approaches, including:

string theory  
loop quantum gravity  
holography  
causal sets  
random geometry  
quantum-information approaches  
emergent-spacetime approaches  

But no complete theory of quantum gravity has been experimentally confirmed as the final answer.

So this repository does not begin by claiming completion.

It begins by preserving the knowns.

## Current Base Rule

A valid theory must preserve what general relativity gets right.

A valid theory must preserve what quantum theory gets right.

A valid theory must explain why they appear incompatible under extreme conditions.

A valid theory must recover time, locality, spacetime, and gravity without assuming them too early.

## Starting Question

What deeper structure could produce both:

continuity / geometry / gravity

and

quantum state / probability / measurement behavior

without destroying either one?

That is the base before shape.

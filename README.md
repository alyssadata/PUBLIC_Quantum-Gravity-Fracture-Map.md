# Current Position

I am not claiming to have solved quantum gravity.

I am building a fracture map.

The known fracture is this:

General relativity treats gravity as dynamical spacetime geometry.
Quantum theory treats physical systems through quantum states, probabilities, fields, and measurement structures that require a stable background or relational replacement for evolution and localization.

The break appears where both must matter at once:
black hole interiors,
Big Bang boundary,
possible Big Crunch boundary,
extreme curvature,
extreme density,
Planck-scale regimes.

Our current claim:

The overlap fails at singularity-boundaries because the stage itself stops being safely separable.

The central unanswered question:

When the stage breaks, what remains stable enough to generate a stage again?

# Current Spine

This file gathers the current spine of the repo.

It separates:

what is known  
what is claimed  
what is proposed  
what remains unanswered  

The purpose is to keep the work restrained.

This repo is not claiming to solve quantum gravity.

It is building a fracture map.

## 1. What is known

General relativity works where it works.

Quantum theory works where it works.

A valid deeper model must preserve both.

Gravity is not optional.

Quantum behavior is not optional.

The overlap becomes most pressured where both general relativity and quantum theory must matter at once:

black hole interiors  
Big Bang boundary  
possible Big Crunch boundary  
extreme curvature  
extreme density  
Planck-scale regimes  

These are the fracture zones.

The first fracture claim is:

**The overlap fails at singularity-boundaries because the stage itself stops being safely separable.**

The failure is not only:

gravity is strong

the stage breaks

## 3. Stage break

The stage is the structure that lets events be placed, compared, measured, and described.

When the stage is stable, physics can ask:

what happens inside space and time?

When the stage breaks, the question changes:

what allows space and time to exist as a usable frame at all?

So this repo moves earlier than ordinary spacetime.

It asks what remains before the stage returns.

## 4. Corrected beginning

The beginning is not:

nothing  
vacuum  
empty space  
a point inside space  
a location  
a container  
a clock  

Those already assume a stage.

The current starting phrase is:

**undifferentiated symmetry**

Meaning:

maximal sameness before difference.

This is not a finished physical claim.

It is a restraint phrase.

It prevents the model from smuggling in space, time, objects, or measure too early.

## 5. First move

The first move is not an object.

The first move is:

**distinction / relation**

A distinction is automatically two-sided:

this / not-this

To distinguish is already to relate.

So distinction and relation may be the same first event seen from two sides.

Distinction gives difference.

Relation gives structured difference.

## 6. Re-entry

One distinction is not yet a world.

The next candidate is:

**re-entry**

Re-entry means:

distinguish the distinction

A distinction becomes available for further distinction.

Difference can repeat.

Relation can fold back into itself.

Pattern can begin.

## 7. Nesting

Repeated distinction may produce nesting.

A later fold contains an earlier fold.

The earlier fold does not contain the later fold.

This creates:

containment asymmetry  
ordered depth  
before / after shape  
sequence  

This is not clock-time.

It is pre-time structure.

## 8. Pre-time and the arrow

Clock-time has not been earned yet.

But an arrow may be earned before a clock.

The current claim is:

**Order gives time something to become.**

Pre-time means:

ordered depth before duration  
sequence without seconds  
arrow without ruler  

The arrow may come from containment asymmetry.

A contained fold and a containing fold are not structurally equivalent.

That asymmetry gives direction.

## 9. The ruler problem

The current edge is the ruler problem.

The current sentence is:

**The arrow may be earned.**

**The amount is still open.**
